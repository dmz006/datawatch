Parent release: datawatch v9.98.1 (phone-friendly Automaton detail tab strip).

**Screen.** The Automaton detail view's tab strip (Overview, Stories, Decisions, Scan and the other tabs; eight in all, including Trace). The web app change is presentation only: on a phone the strip no longer widens the page.

**Behaviour to match in the app.**
- The tab row is one line that scrolls horizontally inside itself; tabs keep their natural width and do not wrap, and the page never scrolls sideways.
- Each tab has a minimum touch height of 40 dp on narrow or touch screens.
- When a tab is selected (or the view opens), the row scrolls so the active tab is centred in view.
- The detail body and its tab body may not grow wider than the screen.

**API contract.** None. No REST, MCP, CLI or chat change, and no new strings (the labels are the existing tab labels in all five locales). A draft handoff is in `docs/plans/parity-issues/ef2f0001.md`.
